Core Overview
 
UEA  is a cross-disciplinary axiomatic framework that reveals the unified underlying logic of all complex systems (physical, biological, psychological, social, technical, civilizational). It breaks disciplinary barriers with 3 irreducible core axioms and 4 formal corollaries, explaining the emergence, adaptation, evolution and collapse of all systems through the core logic of maintaining existence, cost-benefit optimization and dynamic equilibrium.
 
3 Core Axioms (Irreducible & Universal)
 
1. Primacy of Existence: The primary goal of all systems is to maintain their own existence and core identity; all behaviors are subordinate to this demand.
2. Cost-Benefit Optimization Principle: All systems spontaneously minimize existential costs and maximize long-term net benefits (net benefit = total benefit - total cost).
3. Dynamic Equilibrium Attractor: All stable systems evolve toward cost-benefit dynamic equilibrium; this is the optimal state for sustainable existence.
 
4 Formal Corollaries
 
1. Instinct as Optimized Fixed Behavior: Instinct is a low-cost, high-efficiency existential behavior solidified by environmental selection/evolution/design.
2. Environmental Selection as Cost Pressure: Environmental changes that increase costs/reduce benefits form selection pressure; systems must adapt to survive.
3. Existence-Stability Hierarchy: System behavior priority: Avoid extinction → Reduce uncertainty (maintain stability) → Acquire resources/advantages (improve benefits).
4. Sustainable Equilibrium Determines System Lifespan: The wider the equilibrium adaptation range, the stronger the system robustness and the longer its lifespan.

 Key Characteristics
 
- Domain Independence: Applies to material/abstract systems, with/without autonomous consciousness.
- Minimal Hypotheses: 3 core axioms, no additional domain-specific assumptions.
- Empirical Verifiability: Verified through conventional/extreme/special scenarios across 6 core fields.
- Extensibility: Support quantitative modeling and field-specific corollary supplementation (UEA 2.0/3.0 under development).
